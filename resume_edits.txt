FINAL BULLETS
=============

Chronobot  |  TypeScript, Node.js, BullMQ, Redis, PostgreSQL, Prisma, Docker  |  2025

  • Designed and deployed a Discord message scheduler serving 80+ users in TypeScript,
    backed by BullMQ/Redis for async job queuing and PostgreSQL (Prisma) for persistence,
    supporting once, daily, and weekly recurrence with per-user rate limiting

  • Engineered reliable message delivery with idempotency guards to prevent duplicate sends
    on queue re-delivery, 3-attempt exponential backoff, dead-letter tracking, and automatic
    job recovery across bot restarts, reducing silent delivery failures

  • Instrumented with 7 Prometheus metrics (enqueue counters, execution latency histograms,
    schedule-delay tracking), containerized via multi-stage Docker, and validated by a
    223-test Vitest suite in a GitHub Actions CI pipeline
